# 360 brand launch

360 is a coming-soon clothing label whose artwork is built from circles on a regular grid. The site previews concepts and collects interest in the first collection.

## Language

**Design**: One of the four garment concepts, with its own name, garment type and back artwork. A design is not an item available for purchase.

**Colourway**: The black or white version of a garment. Page mode sets every garment’s colourway; a visitor can then choose a different colourway for an individual design.

**Chest mark**: The visitor’s choice of a circle or 360 logo on the wearer’s left chest. It is separate from the full back artwork.

**Garment selection**: The colourway and chest mark chosen for a specific design. Choices persist while the visitor explores the page.

**Garment preview**: A temporary view for inspecting a design from the front or back and looking closer at its artwork. Closing the preview retains the garment selection.

**Campaign photo**: The hero photograph of the World hoodie, paired with its circle-grid world artwork and slogan.

**Lookbook**: The four model photographs showing the collection’s designs and reflecting the visitor’s garment selections.

**Launch signup**: A visitor’s request to receive email updates about the 360 launch, with the displayed consent statement.

**Waitlist**: The saved launch signups. The private preview uses local storage; the published site uses Formspree.
